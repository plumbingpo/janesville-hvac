Food Service Comfort

Comprehensive Food-Service HVAC Systems

Restaurants and food-service businesses have climate demands that ordinary equipment can't meet. Kitchens generate enormous heat loads while dining rooms need to stay comfortable, exhaust hoods pull out conditioned air that makeup air units have to replace, and a busy Friday night pushes rooftop units to their limit. Janesville HVAC specializes in food-service HVAC, including rooftop package units, split systems, correct tonnage and airflow sizing, makeup air balancing against hood exhaust, and scheduled service that works around your hours.

Many restaurant comfort complaints trace back to air balance. When a kitchen runs under negative pressure, it pulls dining-room air the wrong way, sucks hot or cold outdoor air in through every door, and wears out your staff at peak service. We balance the makeup air to the hood exhaust and document every visit in written reports your landlord and auditors can review.

Replacement and maintenance plans are planned alongside owners, managers, and kitchen staff, so everything fits the hours you're open. If a job can't be done during service, we do it after close, keeping your kitchen cooking and guests comfortable in the meantime.

Inspections & Documentation

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Every commercial job we take on starts with safety. We inspect heat exchangers for cracks, verify correct venting and combustion air, test electrical connections and disconnects, verify the refrigerant charge, clear condensate lines, and check ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is straightforward: protecting the people in your building from CO hazards, poor air quality, and water damage. Findings go into a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

The paperwork matters as much as the repair. Every account gets a service binder with detailed service logs and temperature and filter records, giving you proof of due diligence for landlords, insurers, and auditors. Heavy-use equipment, in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms, gets a more frequent visit schedule.

Emergency Service

Fast Commercial HVAC Service

A commercial HVAC failure starts costing money the minute it happens. Kitchens overheat, retail floors empty out, server rooms heat up, and tenants start calling. Our commercial line gets an experienced technician dispatched fast, throughout Janesville and the surrounding area.

Commercial emergencies come in many forms. The cause could be a failed compressor, a burned-out condenser fan motor, a refrigerant leak, a faulty ignition system or gas valve, a tripped high-limit switch, a frozen coil, or a control board stuck in lockout. Whatever it is, we diagnose it fast, restore heating or cooling, and record every step for your facility files. Afterward, we put maintenance and monitoring in place so the same failure doesn't return.

Preventive Care

Commercial Scheduled Maintenance Programs

Stopping failures before they happen is the most cost-effective way to manage commercial HVAC equipment. Every Janesville HVAC program is customized to the property's equipment, usage, and budget, and includes scheduled inspections, coil cleaning, condensate line clearing, filter changes with MERV ratings matched to the space, belt and bearing checks, refrigerant and static pressure testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, and seasonal prep for peak demand.

Scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction of an emergency replacement and significantly reduces the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Our commercial clients consistently report fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower energy bills after implementing a program.
