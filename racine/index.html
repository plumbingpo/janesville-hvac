Full-Service HVAC Care for Racine Homes and Businesses

Heating and cooling around here means a wide mix of equipment: aging furnaces in established neighborhoods, newer heat pumps in recent construction, ductless mini-splits in additions and converted garages, and packaged units on commercial buildings. Our team works on every one of those systems. A typical week for our residential technicians might include capacitor and contactor replacements, flame sensor cleanings, refrigerant leak repairs, blower motor swaps, and at least one full system replacement or ductless install. Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Racine

Many mid-season breakdowns start with maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Change your filter every one to three months, keep plants and shrubs at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and rinse the condenser coil once a year in spring. Then book a professional tune-up twice a year, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Equipment age matters just as much. With regular service, central air typically lasts 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts showing warning signs, like higher energy bills, rooms that never reach the setpoint, or one repair after another, it's time to plan a replacement. Replacing on your own schedule is cheaper and causes far less stress than an emergency swap during the worst heat wave of the year.

Minor issues rarely stay small for long. Take a weak capacitor: replacing it is a short, inexpensive repair, but if it's ignored for a season, it can burn out the compressor, and just like that a minor fix has become a full system replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to save our number in their phone and call at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and prevents a few miserable nights.
